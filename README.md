# Barbara Szabó

**Healthcare Data Scientist | Medical AI | Medical Imaging**

I build reproducible, carefully evaluated AI workflows for healthcare and medical imaging. My work brings together health data science, clinical imaging experience, and practical machine-learning engineering.

I completed an MSc in Health Data Science at Semmelweis University and a research internship at IIS La Fe in Valencia, where I developed 3D MRI segmentation workflows for glioblastoma research.

## Research interests

- 3D medical imaging and multimodal MRI
- Medical image segmentation and model evaluation
- Robustness and trustworthy AI in healthcare
- Clinical decision support and healthcare data quality

## Selected projects

- [RHUH-GBM ET/NECR segmentation audit](https://github.com/barbex21/3D-MRI-Brain-Tumor-Segmentation-Recurrence-Prediction) — Reproducible 3D U-Net workflow and an aggregate comparison of MRI modalities. The repository excludes patient-level and imaging data.
- [Medical imaging adversarial robustness](https://github.com/barbex21/Adversarial_attack) — 3D brain-tumor segmentation and FGSM robustness evaluation.
- [Prostate cancer risk calculator](https://github.com/barbex21/Prostate-Cancer-Risk-Calculator-using-Logistic-Regression) — Logistic-regression demonstration using synthetic data; not a clinical decision tool.
- [Cox survival estimator](https://github.com/barbex21/Cox-based-Survival-and-Life-Expectancy-Estimator) — Survival-analysis demonstration using synthetic data; not for clinical use.
- [Personalized eyewear measurement](https://github.com/barbex21/Real-time-face-recognition-glasses-fitting) — Computer-vision workflow for facial landmarks and customized 3D-printed frames.
